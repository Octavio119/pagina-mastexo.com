# Constructor de Cartas de Ventas

## Estructura de Email de Venta

```
SUBJECT LINE: [Específico + curiosidad]

APERTURA: [Personalization + hook]

CUERPO: [Problem → Solution → Outcome]

CTA: [Specific action + urgency]

CIERRE: [Signature]
```

## Ejemplo

```
SUBJECT: [Name], are you leaving $50K on the table?

Hi [Name],

I was looking at your agency's website and noticed something:
You're serving clients but might not be positioning premium pricing.

Most agencies leave 40-60% on the table because they under-price.

We help agencies raise their rates without losing clients.
In fact, most of our clients increased rates 30-50% in 90 days.

Want to chat about how? Reply with "demo" and let's talk.

Best,
[Your name]
```

---

## Restricciones

- **NUNCA genérico** — personalizado
- **NUNCA largo** — máx 100 palabras en cuerpo
- **NUNCA sin CTA** — siempre acción específica
