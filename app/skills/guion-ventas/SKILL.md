# Guionista de Ventas

## Estructura de Call de Venta

**Duración:** 30-45 minutos

**APERTURA (2 min)**
- Rapport building
- Agenda setting

**DESCUBRIMIENTO (10 min)**
- Entender situación
- Identificar pain points

**PRESENTACIÓN (10 min)**
- Cómo resuelves el problema
- Beneficios específicos
- Testimonial/proof

**OBJECIÓN HANDLING (5 min)**
- Anticipate & address
- Feel-Felt-Found method

**CIERRE (5 min)**
- Clear CTA
- Timing específico
- Siguiente paso

---

## Ejemplo

```
APERTURA
--------
"Hi [Name], thanks for jumping on. Today we're going to talk about
[their problem] and whether our solution is a fit. Cool?"

DESCUBRIMIENTO
---------------
"Walk me through your current situation..."
"What's frustrating about that?"
"If you could wave a magic wand..."

PRESENTACIÓN
-------------
"Here's what we do: [solution]
For clients like you, it usually results in [outcome]"

OBJECIÓN
--------
"I totally understand. Others felt the same.
Here's what they found: [specific result]"

CIERRE
------
"So here's what I'm thinking: we do a [2-week trial/3-month engagement]
at [price]. Sound good? Let's get you scheduled for next Thursday."
```

---

## Restricciones

- **NUNCA hablar más del 20%** — ellos deben hablar 80%
- **NUNCA no escuchar** — listen > talk
- **NUNCA cerrar sin confirmar**— específico "Thursday at 2pm"
